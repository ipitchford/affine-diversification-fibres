## Questions for final reviewers

1. **Fixed-stem theorem:** Are the conditioning normalisers and event conventions in Theorems 3–4 correct for the stated oriented-ranked-tree, fixed-stem, stem-survival and fixed-tip-count setting?
2. **Sharpness and feasibility:** Do Theorems 6 and 8 and Corollaries 7 and 9–11 prove the claimed endpoint attainment, barrier conditions and minimum-cap formula over exactly the stated model class?
3. **Semantic bridge:** Is the frozen bridge `rho=1`, `lambda_ref=lambda_p`, `mu_ref=0` a fair and correctly described common comparison surface for the selected CRABS procedures?
4. **Structural censors:** Is it acceptable to count the 60 primary abrupt-signal censors as H2 events under the prospectively documented amendment, given that the manuscript also excludes them from H4 and never calls them returned-cloud estimates?
5. **Decision utility:** Are the 16 preset queries per noncensored cell, pooled H4 rule and zero-false-certificate condition scientifically interpretable? Does the paper avoid subgroup rescue after the pooled failure?
6. **Scope and priority:** Is “exact diagnostic complement” appropriately calibrated across macroevolution, stochastic-process, partial-identification and numerical-certification audiences?
7. **Reproducibility and rights:** Is the evidence sufficient for external replay, and what release-specific licensing or redistribution action is required for the CRABS-derived qualified signal and result artefacts?
