2. **Hallucinated citation or result — clear.** All citations map to the source registry; all new numbers map to sealed JSON. The invalid inherited ISBN was detected and corrected rather than ignored.

Amendment 004 followed the bounded nontermination incident but preceded the complete Stage-2 rerun and every primary H2/H4 result; it changed no seed, cell, threshold or replacement rule.
