# Submission workplan after candidate 0.2.0

## Decision

Do not add another broad empirical example before securing the central theorem and priority boundaries. The highest-value route is a staged methods-paper programme.

## Stage 1: secure the theorem core

1. Commission an independent stochastic-process audit of Theorem 4, with a written derivation of the fixed-stem normalisation and an explicit verdict on every event convention.
2. Run a recognition search across splitting trees, Crump-Mode-Jagers processes, survival analysis, compensators, Stieltjes measures, viability/optimal recovery, dissertations and non-English sources.
3. Formalise Theorems 1, 6 and 8, or obtain a fully independent proof reconstruction.

**Promotion criterion:** no load-bearing error and no antecedent that absorbs the full residual theorem package.

## Stage 2: establish practical significance

1. Run CRABS or Andréoletti-Morlon under restrictions matched exactly to the analytic fibre.
2. Report sample size, proposal mechanism, tuning and runtime.
3. Compare the finite sampled cloud with the exact endpoints and certificate.
4. Identify at least one substantive conclusion that a finite cloud leaves uncertified or gets materially wrong.

**Promotion criterion:** the exact method changes, secures or substantially accelerates a decision-relevant conclusion.

## Stage 3: choose the inferential scope

### Theory-only route

Keep all results conditional on a fixed pulled signal. Remove any residual palaeobiological language that could be read as inference. This is sufficient for a strong applied-mathematics/methods paper.

### Full inferential route

Construct a simultaneous uncertainty set for the pulled signal and propagate it through the fibre. Add uncertainty in sampling fraction, external constraints and event timing. Demonstrate coverage by simulation.

### Palaeontological route

Specify a preservation/detection model and a taxonomic-scale bridge before using fossil summaries as constraints. Compare with identifiable fossilised birth-death inference.

## High-value mathematical extensions

- Robust envelopes over a family of pulled signals.
- Time-varying turnover caps and uncertain event ages.
- Exact optimisation of non-order-monotone targets such as peak timing.
- Crown and random-origin finite-event laws.
- Matrix/operator-valued analogues for lineage or state heterogeneity.

## Stop or narrow conditions

Stop or substantially narrow if an external audit finds an uncancelled conditioning factor, prior art contains the same coupled fibre-and-certificate theory, uncertainty makes the sets vacuous in realistic settings, or matched samplers recover all relevant endpoints more cheaply.
