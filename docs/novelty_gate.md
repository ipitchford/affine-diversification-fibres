# Novelty gate, release 0.2.0

**Search freeze:** 6 August 2026  
**Decision:** proceed as a bounded candidate; specialist recognition still required.

## Corpus boundary

The gate inspected the modern diversification-congruence literature, identifiable restricted birth-death models, reconstructed-process and coalescent-point-process work with incomplete sampling and survival events, generic Lipschitz extension, infinite-dimensional linear programming, partial identification, and recent fossilised or hidden-event identifiability work. Searches used publisher/DOI pages, PubMed, arXiv/bioRxiv, openly available full text and exact formula/title searches. It was targeted rather than systematic and did not cover every book, thesis, subscription database, language or adjacent terminology.

- Louca and Pennell's congruence classes and pulled variables.
- The integrating-factor/Bernoulli linearisation.
- Lambert and Stadler's inverse-tail/coalescent-point-process coordinate and sampling transformations.
- Höhna's event-aware reconstructed birth-death process.
- Zero-inflated geometric linear birth-death descendant laws.
- CRABS and Andréoletti-Morlon exploration of congruent histories.
- Legried and Terhorst's identifiable restricted classes.
- Generic McShane/Whitney Lipschitz envelopes and infinite-dimensional linear programming.
- Fossilised and hidden-birth-event routes to restored identifiability.

## Residual priority claim

No located source combined all of the following in the same fixed-pulled-signal framework:

1. a cumulative-loss positive measure beneath the explicit barrier `A < F`;
2. reciprocal speciation affine in that measure and turnover as `dnu/dF`;
3. a fixed-stem finite-event congruence theorem stated through the full descendant-count law and normalized tree density;
4. all-cap sharp bounds with the `c=1` phase transition and supercritical divergence scale;
5. finite directed-Lipschitz interval certificates, the positive-survival test and minimum compatible cap;
6. an exact sample-complexity theorem for random endpoint exploration.

The originality lies in this coupled theorem system and its model-specific consequences. None of the constituent generic mathematical devices is claimed as new.

These are subjective research-priority probabilities, not frequencies.
