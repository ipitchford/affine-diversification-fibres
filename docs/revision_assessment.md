## Why this is not yet 4*

A 4* case would require several of the following:

- an independent specialist confirms the event-conditioned tree law and conditioning conventions;
- a stronger recognition search fails to locate the same measure fibre and certificate package;
- a matched CRABS or alternative implementation shows that certified extrema change or secure a substantive conclusion;
- statistical uncertainty over the pulled signal is propagated with simultaneous coverage;
- a defensible fossil observation model turns the biological illustration into inference rather than sensitivity;
- the central analytic theorems receive formal or independent proof-level verification.
