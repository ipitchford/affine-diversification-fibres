## Additional mathematical gain

The revision did more than narrow claims. It added three results that materially improve the original work:

1. a fully normalised fixed-stem finite-event theorem;
2. an all-cap phase theory identifying when upper rate bounds cease to be finite;
3. an exact endpoint-sampling law that converts the qualitative critique of sampled clouds into a dimensional sample-complexity statement.

## Remaining release blockers

The candidate should not be promoted until at least the external conditioning audit, specialist novelty recognition and either an actual matched-method comparison or a clear editorial waiver are complete. Statistical and fossil-observation gates may instead be handled by permanently narrowing the paper to conditional theory, but the present package keeps them visible as research extensions.
