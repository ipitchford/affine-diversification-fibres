# Sources

The source registry separates three roles:

1. reconstructed-process and coalescent-point-process antecedents for the fixed-stem law;
2. statistical and mathematical antecedents for DKW--Massart concentration, partial-identification confidence sets, Lipschitz extension, optimal recovery and product integration;
3. CRABS and related congruence tools used only for the bounded comparator study.

Bibliographic identities and role descriptions are in `SOURCES.json` and `review/route_a/SOURCES_ROUTE_A.json`. Full formatted references are in `references.bib` and the manuscript bibliography. Source checks establish identity and contextual support; they do not validate the candidate's deductions.
