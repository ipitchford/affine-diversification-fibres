### Points of disagreement and resolution

#### Disagreement 1: Must the revision construct a complete statistical procedure for `F`?

- **EIC/R3 view:** A Systematic Biology or uptake-oriented paper needs an uncertainty-aware, end-to-end worked analysis.
- **R1/R2 view:** The conditional theorem paper may remain valuable if its scope is made exact and the empirical example is not used as validation.
- **Type:** Direction disagreement.
- **Editor's resolution:** The revision must choose and declare one of two routes. **Route A, broad methods route:** construct an honest simultaneous uncertainty set for `F`, propagate it through the certificate and demonstrate a consequential target. **Route B, theorem route:** make the paper explicitly conditional and theorem-led, demote the mammal study to supplementary sensitivity, and remove any implication of end-to-end biological inference. Given the stated goal of broad cross-field acceptance, Route A is preferred, but a well-executed Route B can still support a narrower Evidence Press candidate.

#### Disagreement 2: Does the H2 censor rule invalidate the benchmark?

- **R1/DA view:** A censor is not an observed endpoint deficit, and Amendment 004 was outcome-informed.
- **EIC/R3 view:** The benchmark remains informative if the two outcome types and amendment timing are transparent.
- **Type:** Severity disagreement.
- **Editor's resolution:** H2 is not discarded because all 240 returned clouds independently meet the deficit threshold, so the pass does not rely on counting the 60 censors. The manuscript must nevertheless rename the composite and report `240/240 returned-cloud deficits` plus `60/60 structural censors` before presenting the frozen 300/300 workflow result.

#### Disagreement 3: Are the pooled H4 Wilson intervals acceptable?

- **R1 view:** The 3,840 statuses are dependent within cells, so the Wilson range has no conventional query-population interpretation.
- **EIC view:** It may remain as a visibly descriptive aid.
- **Type:** Severity disagreement.
- **Editor's resolution:** The exact frozen gate statistic 532/3,840 remains authoritative. The pooled Wilson range may appear only if labelled non-inferential and accompanied by the 3-by-3 status transition table plus cell/stratum summaries. It must not be described as field-wide uncertainty.

#### Disagreement 4: Is successor packaging part of scientific review?

- **EIC/R3 view:** Conflicting 0.2.1 and 0.3.0 narratives are a major Evidence Press readiness problem.
- **R1/R2 view:** Packaging does not determine theorem truth.
- **Type:** Perspective difference.
- **Editor's resolution:** Packaging is not evidence for the proof, but it is a mandatory publication gate for an evidence-led release. It therefore remains a required revision in the Evidence Press roadmap and must be verified separately from the scientific re-review.

### Decision rationale

The article contains a potentially publishable theoretical contribution: it turns a fixed-signal homogeneous diversification congruence class into an explicit conditional identified set with sharp cap-regime bounds, finite feasibility certificates and endpoint constructions. Its internal computational assurance is extensive, and the authors' decision to retain the failed H4 utility gate materially strengthens credibility. Those strengths justify “resubmit encouraged” rather than rejection.

Major revision is required because the word “exact” places a high burden on model-class and probability-space specification. Theorem 1's regularity is not internally matched, and Theorem 4 presently proves an ordered-time topology-marginal density while describing a complete reconstructed-tree law. The benchmark is complete at the ledger level, but H2 conflates an observed deficit with structural nonreturn, H4 lacks transition directions, and its descriptive query-level interval is vulnerable to pseudo-replication. The paper also stops before a simultaneous uncertainty procedure for the pulled signal or a fossil observation model, so broad biological uptake is not established. Finally, independent theorem review, external replay, cross-platform evaluation, systematic priority search and successor rights/metadata remain open. These are substantial but tractable repairs; none requires erasing the negative H4 outcome or historical receipts.

### Required item details and acceptance criteria

#### R1: Contribution route and claim hierarchy

- **Problem:** The paper combines a theorem system, a software safeguard, a comparator study and an empirical illustration without one dominant publication claim.
- **Requirement:** Select Route A or B; rewrite the title, abstract, contribution list, discussion and conclusion around that route. Keep H4 failed and prohibit essential-complement wording.
- **Acceptance criteria:** A reader can state the paper's one principal contribution and current use in two sentences; every secondary component is labelled support, illustration or future work.

#### R2: Theorem 1 regularity

- **Problem:** `lambda_p` continuous and `A in C^1` do not automatically yield continuously differentiable rate functions.
- **Requirement:** Define the domain/codomain function spaces precisely or strengthen assumptions, then propagate the definition to the claim ledger and implementation documentation.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Forward and inverse maps land in the stated spaces with no unstated differentiability step.

#### R3: Theorem 4 probability object

- **Problem:** The equations are topology-marginal while the theorem claims a complete tree law.
- **Requirement:** Define stem/survival/tip conditioning, event-side convention, labels, ranking, orientation and base measure. Give the topology probability or narrow the theorem title. Obtain written independent process-theory review.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Every combinatorial factor is derivable under one explicit convention; the density integrates/sums to one; the external reviewer finds no unresolved major conditioning error.

#### R4: Model-class and biological restrictions

- **Problem:** Singular-continuous measures, finite events, time-only homogeneity and the biological consequences of `mu/lambda <= c` are not presented as one explicit applicability map.
- **Requirement:** Add a model-class table and cap-regime interpretation, including what `c<=1` excludes and which process results cover which measure subclasses.
- **Acceptance criteria:** No reader can reasonably apply the result to lineage/state-dependent models or interpret an event atom as a continuous turnover value without encountering an explicit warning.

#### R5: H2 and protocol chronology

- **Problem:** A structural censor is grouped with an observed returned-cloud deficit, and the post-incident timing can be misread.
- **Requirement:** Add a dated timeline; identify Amendment 004 as informed by Stage-1 nontermination but frozen before primary rejection results; report 240/240 returned deficits and 60 censors separately; rename the composite workflow result.
- **Acceptance criteria:** The scientific H2 conclusion is true without censors, and the 300/300 number is never described as 300 observed cloud deficits.

#### R6: H4 interpretation

- **Problem:** The direction and clustering of 532 status changes are hidden by one pooled percentage.
- **Requirement:** Add the 3-by-3 status transition table, per-cell and per-stratum summaries, censor denominator, and a clear explanation of dependency. Call zero false certificates an implementation consistency invariant unless tested against an independent oracle.
- **Acceptance criteria:** A reader can reconstruct every H4 status direction and cannot mistake the Wilson display for a general field-wide confidence interval.

#### R7: Statistical/biological bridge

- **Problem:** Exact conditional projection is not an operational inferential method.
- **Requirement, Route A:** Construct an honest simultaneous uncertainty set for `F`, propagate it, and demonstrate one target-specific decision. **Requirement, Route B:** remove any suggestion of current statistical coverage or palaeobiological inference and make the mammal material supplementary sensitivity only.
- **Acceptance criteria:** The manuscript's claims match the chosen route; no plug-in curve is presented as an uncertainty-aware inference.

#### R8: Recognition and literature

- **Problem:** The priority claim is based on a targeted 18-source corpus.
- **Requirement:** Search the planned adjacent literatures and create a theorem-by-theorem antecedent table. Incorporate the specific domain references listed in Reviewer 2's report.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Each claimed contribution is classified as antecedent, reformulation, extension or new synthesis, with search dates and databases recorded; residual priority uncertainty is explicit.

#### R9: Reproducible successor release

- **Problem:** The candidate mixes historical 0.2.1 instructions with 0.3.0 review materials and lacks independent full recreation.
- **Requirement:** Create a successor README, citation/metadata, component-level licence map, deterministic environments, one-command replay, resource budget, Linux/macOS checks and an unaffiliated reproduction receipt. Preserve all historical receipts unchanged.
- **Acceptance criteria:** A fresh checkout can regenerate and validate the scientific summaries; rights are explicit for every redistributed CRABS-derived component; no successor file claims the 0.2.1 DOI.
