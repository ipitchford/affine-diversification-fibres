# Stage 3 review configuration

**Manuscript:** *Conditional Sharp Partial Identification of Diversification Histories: Affine Measure Geometry, Event Congruence, Certified Extremes and a Confirmatory CRABS Comparison*

**Candidate:** 0.3.0-candidate, 21 August 2026

**Review mode:** Full, five-perspective, first-round review

**Review boundary:** Internal simulated expert review. This exercise does not count as external peer review, specialist theorem certification, independent replay, community acceptance or publication authorization.

## Field analysis

- **Primary field:** systematic biology and time-varying phylogenetic birth-death modelling.
- **Secondary fields:** stochastic-process theory, partial identification, infinite-dimensional optimisation and reproducible computational science.
- **Research paradigm:** theorem-led conceptual/methodological paper with a prospective computational comparator study.
- **Method type:** analytic proof, exact finite-constraint certification, stochastic benchmark and reproducibility audit.
- **Maturity:** technically advanced candidate, but missing external theorem review, an operational uncertainty procedure for the pulled signal, an observation-aware fossil bridge and independent replay.
- **Target tier:** field-leading specialist standard. Systematic Biology is the primary editorial lens; Methods in Ecology and Evolution is the uptake-oriented alternative; Theoretical Population Biology is the mathematical alternative.

## Confirmed independent reviewer roles

1. **Editor-in-Chief:** senior systematic-biology editor specialising in theoretical macroevolution; assesses fit, originality, significance, accessibility and claim calibration.
2. **Reviewer 1 — Methodology:** mathematical statistician working in partial identification, measure-valued systems and optimisation; audits theorem statements, proof logic, sharpness, benchmark design and statistical reporting.
3. **Reviewer 2 — Domain:** stochastic phylogenetics specialist working on reconstructed birth-death processes and conditioning; audits event semantics, topology/branching-time laws, biological meaning and scholarly positioning.
4. **Reviewer 3 — Perspective:** computational macroevolution and reproducible-software specialist familiar with congruence-class exploration; audits practical use, CRABS comparability, censoring, replayability, portability and rights.
5. **Devil's Advocate:** identifiability and meta-science specialist; constructs the strongest case that the exact conditional geometry may remain scientifically non-actionable and tests the logic of the benchmark and contribution claim.

## Independence rule

Each Phase 1 report was prepared from the manuscript and evidence packet without using another Phase 1 report. The editorial synthesis was prepared only after all five reports existed. Reviewer comments are not manuscript edits.
